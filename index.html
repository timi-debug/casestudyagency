The client

Cem Atik runs Harucon Ventures out of Düsseldorf, a marketing agency for e-commerce brands. He sells to those brands and the people who own them.

The figures on this page come from his own reporting to us and from the account itself.

Where he started

About 5,000 followers and already publishing regularly. Advice, mostly. People read it and moved on.

Nothing in his calendar was coming from any of it.

What we ran

  1. Wrote the briefCompany type, size, the person who signs and what a client worth keeping looks like. Every meeting was later checked against it.
  2. Rebuilt the profileIn the words his buyers use when they describe the problem he solves, not the words agencies use to describe themselves.
  3. Built the target listsOne per buyer type, gated by company type, size and geography.
  4. Sent connection requests with no messageOnce a request is accepted, what he publishes shows up in that person's feed.
  5. Changed what went outHis own client results instead of advice. What the client came in with, what was done, and what it returned.
  6. Sent one message per contactOnly after the posts had been running, so it arrived from someone they already recognised.
  7. Qualified every reply in the inboxCompany, budget, timing and who signs. Only the ones that cleared the brief went to his calendar.

What changed

Before

Advice posts, written for everyone. An audience that agreed with him and did nothing. New work arriving through his own network, when it arrived.

After

His own client results, written for one buyer. That buyer in the audience on purpose. One message each, sent after they already knew who he was.

What it took from him

A working session to set the brief, approval of the posts before they went out, and the calls themselves. We wrote the posts, built the lists, sent the requests and messages, and handled the inbox.

No ads. No cold email.
